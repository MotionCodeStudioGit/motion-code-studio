What an adjustment really does

A chiropractic adjustment restores movement to joints that have become restricted, easing pain, releasing tension, and helping your body function more freely. Done well, it's precise, controlled, and tailored to you.

At Motion Code Studio, Dr. Maksim Ishankhanov, DC, treats the adjustment as one part of a bigger picture: relief now, paired with the corrective movement that makes it last.

Restored motion

Freeing restricted joints so you move with less pain and more ease.

Pain relief

Easing the mechanical strain behind back, neck, and joint pain.

Better function

Helping your body move and feel the way it's meant to.

Tailored technique

Adjustments matched to your body, history, and comfort.

Relief that lasts

An adjustment can bring fast relief, but without addressing why the restriction formed, it tends to return. That's why every adjustment here is paired with corrective work, so progress holds between visits.

FAQ

Common questions

Does an adjustment hurt?

No. Adjustments are precise and controlled. Many clients feel immediate relief and easier movement; some feel mild, brief soreness afterward, similar to after exercise.

Is it safe?

Chiropractic adjustment is a well-established, conservative approach. After a full assessment, care is tailored to your body and any specific considerations.

How often will I need adjustments?

It depends on your goals and what we're addressing. We'll give you an honest plan rather than open-ended visits, with the aim of lasting results.
