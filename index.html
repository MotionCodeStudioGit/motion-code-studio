The Approach

We combine integrative bodywork with advanced chiropractic care to restore movement throughout the body. Each session builds on the one before it, continuing your personalized plan and progressing steadily toward your results, rather than treating every visit as a standalone fix. Our deliberately planned, hands-on bodywork techniques and precise chiropractic adjustments work together to reduce pain and support long-term physical performance, whether you’re recovering from injury or maintaining peak condition.

Dr. Maksim Ishankhanov, DC·Founder & Lead Practitioner

What we practice

Five parts of one philosophy

Most people are taught to think about their body only when something hurts. We work the other way, building the structure, tissue, capability, and understanding that keep you well before pain starts.

Chiropractic care

Restores structure. Precise, hands-on treatment of the joints and spine, tailored to what your assessment finds.

Integrative bodywork

Restores healthy tissue. Deep, skilled massage and soft-tissue work that releases what holds the structure out of place.

Movement

Develops capability. Corrective work that turns new range into strength you keep, so progress does not reverse.

Recovery

Supports adaptation. Decompression and restorative work that let the body absorb the change and rebuild.

Education

Creates understanding. We explain what we find in plain terms, so you can care for your body between visits.

FAQ

Common questions

Do I need a referral to book?

No. You can book directly. Every plan starts with an assessment, and we recommend the right care from there.

What happens at the first visit?

A consultation and health history, then a hands-on, full-body movement evaluation. We explain what we find in plain terms and lay out the recommended plan before any treatment begins.

How long is a session?

Sessions run 30, 60, or 90 minutes. The longer sessions allow time for the full-treatment work, including the Motion Room where it fits your plan.

Do you come to me?

Yes. Concierge appointments are available across Greater Los Angeles, at your home, office, or hotel. Concierge fees include travel within 10 miles of the Burbank studio.

Will I be using the Motion Room?

Only if it suits you. The Motion Frame is one of several tools and is used after an evaluation, where it is appropriate for your body and your plan.

What are your hours?
