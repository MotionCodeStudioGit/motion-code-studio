Why it keeps coming back

Back pain is rarely just about your back

Pain in one place is often the result of many things working together: how your joints move, where muscles are tight or overworked, the demands of your daily movement, old injuries, and the physical stress of how you sit, stand, and work.

Treating only the spot that hurts tends to bring relief that fades. A whole-body assessment looks for what is actually driving the pain, so the plan addresses the cause and not only the symptom.

The path

A clear four-step roadmap

Care is structured and progressive, so you understand each stage and why it comes when it does.

1. Evaluate

A full-body movement assessment to find the restrictions and patterns contributing to your pain.

2. Restore

Hands-on care to release tension and return mobility to the joints and tissue that need it.

3. Align

Precise chiropractic adjustment once the surrounding tissue has released, so the correction holds.

4. Stabilize

Building the strength and control to hold the gains, so relief lasts instead of returning.

About Dr. Max

Dr. Maksim Ishankhanov, DC, known to his patients as Dr. Max, is a highly trained chiropractor known for his precise, results-driven approach to care. He earned his medical degree from Moscow State Medical and Stomatological University, bringing a deeper clinical understanding of the body beyond standard chiropractic training.

Unlike high-volume clinics, Dr. Max works one-on-one with every patient, taking the time to assess, treat, and adjust with intention. His sessions are focused, hands-on, and tailored, with a level of attention that goes beyond the typical appointment.

That precision comes from years of hands-on clinical practice and a full-body philosophy: pain in one place is usually driven by restriction somewhere else, so he reads the whole body before treating the spot that hurts.

Patients trust him for more than temporary relief: lasting improvement in pain, mobility, and overall function. His goal is simple, fix the root of the problem, not just the symptoms.
