Your First Session

Four simple steps

Every first visit follows the same clear arc. You are never rushed, and you always understand what is happening and why.

1 · Before you arrive

Book online in a couple of taps. Wear comfortable clothing you can move in, and arrive a few minutes early to settle in.

2 · Assessment

We look at how you move, where you are restricted, your history, and what is actually driving the issue, before anyone touches the table.

3 · Hands-on care

If an adjustment is right for you, you are treated the same day, paired with corrective movement so the change holds.

4 · Your plan

You leave with a clear path forward and simple corrective work to do between visits, not a vague "come back next week."

FAQ

First-visit questions

What should I wear to my first visit?

Comfortable clothing you can move in. We assess how you move, so anything that lets you bend, reach, and sit easily works well.

How long is the first appointment?

Plan for your full session. The first visit includes a thorough assessment along with hands-on care, so it runs a little longer than a typical follow-up.

Will I be adjusted on the first visit?

Usually yes. After the assessment, if an adjustment is appropriate for you, Dr. Ishankhanov will treat the same day and explain what he is doing and why.

How do I pay, and do you take insurance?

Motion Code Studio is a private, self-pay studio, so we do not bill insurance. You will see clear session pricing when you book. We accept all major payment methods, including credit and debit cards, Zelle, and Venmo, collected at the time of service.

How does a concierge visit work?

For concierge sessions, Dr. Ishankhanov brings care to you anywhere across Greater Los Angeles, at home or at the office. Fees include travel within 10 miles of the Burbank studio, with a tailored travel fee beyond that.
