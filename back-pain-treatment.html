Get to the root of your back pain

Back pain is rarely just about the back. A desk-bound posture, an old injury, weak supporting muscles, or a single wrong movement can leave your spine compensating in ways that build into chronic discomfort. At Motion Code Studio, Dr. Maksim Ishankhanov, DC, reads the full pattern before treating it.

Care combines hands-on chiropractic adjustment with corrective movement, so relief holds instead of returning the moment you leave the table. The goal is a back that moves freely and stays that way.

Lower back pain

Stiffness, aching, or sharp pain in the lumbar spine from sitting, lifting, or strain.

Upper back & tension

Tightness between the shoulder blades, often tied to posture and desk work.

Disc & nerve irritation

Pain that radiates or flares with movement, assessed and managed conservatively.

Recurring strain

Backs that 'go out' repeatedly. We address the why, not just the episode.

What to expect

Your first visit is a thorough assessment: how you move, where you're restricted, and what's driving the pain. From there you get a clear plan: adjustments to restore motion, plus specific corrective work you can build on between sessions.

FAQ

Common questions

How many sessions will I need for back pain?

It depends on how long the issue has been building and whether it's acute or chronic. Many clients feel meaningful relief within the first few visits; lasting change comes from consistent care plus the corrective movement built into your plan.

Is chiropractic care safe for back pain?

Yes. Chiropractic adjustment is a well-established, conservative approach to mechanical back pain. After a full assessment, Dr. Ishankhanov tailors care to your body and history.

Do you offer concierge visits for back pain?

Yes. Concierge sessions bring care to you anywhere in Greater Los Angeles, and in-studio sessions are available in Burbank.
