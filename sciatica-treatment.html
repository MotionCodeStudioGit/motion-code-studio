Understanding sciatica

Sciatica is pain along the sciatic nerve, typically a sharp, burning, or radiating sensation that travels from the lower back or glute down the back of the leg. It's a symptom, not a diagnosis, and the real cause can range from a compressed nerve root to tight muscles pressing on the nerve.

At Motion Code Studio, Dr. Maksim Ishankhanov, DC, identifies what's actually irritating the nerve, then builds a conservative plan to take pressure off it and keep it off.

Radiating leg pain

Pain, burning, or shooting sensations from the low back into the buttock and leg.

Numbness & tingling

Pins-and-needles or numbness along the nerve path, assessed carefully.

Tight, overactive muscles

Glute and hip tension that can compress the nerve, released through targeted work.

Flare-ups with sitting

Pain that worsens with prolonged sitting or certain positions.

A conservative path to relief

Care focuses on reducing nerve irritation through adjustment, mobility, and targeted corrective movement, not masking it. You'll leave each visit with a clear sense of progress and tools to manage flare-ups between sessions.

FAQ

Common questions

How long does sciatica take to improve?

It varies with the cause and how long it's been present. Many clients see steady improvement over a short course of conservative care, paired with movement work that keeps pressure off the nerve.

Do I need surgery for sciatica?

Most sciatica responds well to conservative care and never requires surgery. We always start with the least invasive, evidence-based approach and refer out if anything suggests you need it.

Can you come to me?

Yes. Concierge sessions are available across Greater Los Angeles, which many sciatica clients prefer when sitting or travel aggravates the pain.
