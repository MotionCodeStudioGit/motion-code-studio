Often, yes. When headaches stem from neck and upper-back tension, restoring motion and easing muscle strain can reduce both frequency and intensity. We assess to confirm the source first.

No. Neck adjustments are gentle and precise. Many clients feel an immediate sense of release and easier movement afterward.

Many people notice more freedom of movement within the first visit or two, with lasting change building over a short course of care.
