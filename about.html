The practitioner

Precise, hands-on, results-driven

Dr. Max is a highly trained chiropractor known for his precise, results-driven approach to care. He earned his medical degree from Moscow State Medical and Stomatological University, bringing a deeper clinical understanding of the body beyond standard chiropractic training.

Unlike high-volume clinics, Dr. Max works one-on-one with every patient, taking the time to assess, treat, and adjust with intention. His sessions are focused, hands-on, and tailored, with a level of attention that goes beyond the typical appointment.

His sessions combine deep fascial and soft-tissue work with precise chiropractic adjustment, movement and postural restoration. The hands-on bodywork is a large part of what he actually does, not an add-on to the adjustment.

Patients trust him for more than temporary relief: lasting improvement in pain, mobility, and overall function.

Bodywork & movement

Tissue, mobility, and how the body moves

Edgar combines therapeutic bodywork with an extensive background in movement to help clients restore mobility, reduce persistent muscular and fascial tension, and improve how their bodies move and function.

His integrative bodywork sessions may include massage therapy, deep tissue work, myofascial release, trigger-point techniques, cupping, assisted stretching, postural restoration, and movement-based work tailored to the individual.

His movement approach focuses on restoring joint function, improving active range of motion, developing mobility and stability, and building greater control through usable ranges of movement.

With a lifelong background in professional dance and movement practice, Edgar approaches the body as an interconnected system of tissue, joints, posture, mobility, stability, and coordination, rather than treating one isolated area at a time.

Edgar Khachaturov · Certified Massage Therapist & Movement Practitioner

The philosophy

Fix the root of the problem, not just the symptoms

Pain in one place is often driven by restriction somewhere else. Every plan starts with a full-body assessment, built on years of hands-on clinical practice: how you move, where you are restricted, and what your daily life asks of your body. Treatment follows what the assessment finds, hands-on care first.
